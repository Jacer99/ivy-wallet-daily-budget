1. Liquid Glass & Ambient Glow UI Primitives:
   - `Modifier.pocketMoneyBackground(isDark)`: Reusable 3-stop radial
     mesh background (Dark: #0B0B14 cosmic mesh; Light: #F4F3F8 pearl mesh)
     with 400ms cross-fade animation.
   - `Modifier.specularBorder(shape, isDark)`: 0.5dp top-down lighting
     gradient rim simulating refraction.
   - `LiquidGlassCard`: Adaptive composable container with translucent
     fill (obsidian in dark, crisp pearl in light with ambient shadow).

     brush, twin cards use Nordic Moss / Burnished Espresso gradients,
